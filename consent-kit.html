Key information

The purpose of the research is to find the effective dose range of YKP3089 as additional therapy for partial seizures, and to evaluate how safe it is and how well people handle it.

Teach-back questions

Asked in the participant’s own words, after the discussion and before signing.

  1. In your own words, what is this study testing?
  2. What are the chances you would get placebo?
  3. What would you do if you noticed a rash?
  4. Why shouldn’t you stop the study drug suddenly?
  5. What should you do if you want to leave the study?

If an answer is off, I explain that part again and re-ask. Signing waits until every answer is right.

Judgment calls

Decisions a template can’t make for you.

Benefits without numbers

I left earlier-study efficacy figures out of the benefits section. “Your seizures may become less frequent, but this is not guaranteed” says it without sounding like a promise.

Checking alertness first

People with epilepsy can be drowsy after a seizure or from medication. The note records that the participant was alert before the discussion began.

Placeholders on a public document

Site, investigator, and phone numbers are placeholders, so a mock form never implies a real person’s involvement or sends anyone to a real line.
