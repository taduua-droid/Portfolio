Protocol operations packet

I turned a 163-page Phase 2 epilepsy protocol into the documents a site needs to run it: a one-page synopsis, visit-by-visit checklists with calculated windows, and an eligibility checklist.

Why this protocol

I chose this protocol because it is my field of interests of research in both Neurology and epilepsy studies. It also expands over trials similar to the roles I’m applying for, and its 10 visits, two-step dose plan, and diary-based eligibility make for real coordination work.

Visit 7, maintenance

Participant 001 (synthetic)

Target Day 71. Window Day 68 to 74. Plan for a 2 to 4 hour visit.

  • Confirm today falls within Day 68 to 74
  • First YKP3089 blood sample on arrival; record the time
  • Other seizure-drug trough levels, timed to the last dose
  • Vital signs: supine 5 minutes, then standing 3 minutes; weight
  • C-SSRS, Since Last Visit version
  • Brief neurologic exam by a delegated investigator
  • Collect and review seizure diary; dispense a new one
  • Count returned tablets; dispense the next blister card
  • Review adverse events and medication changes; escalate new events to the PI
  • Second YKP3089 sample 2 to 4 hours after the first
  • Book Visit 8 inside Day 96 to 102

Judgment calls

What a checklist can’t show: the places in this protocol where running the study takes planning, and what I did about them.

Verify with CRA

Two blood draws, hours apart

Visit 7 needs a second YKP3089 sample 2 to 4 hours after the first, plus trough levels of the participant’s other seizure drugs. I scheduled it as a long morning visit and told the participant ahead of time.

Verify with CRA

Eligibility is counted, not checked

Participants need at least 8 qualifying seizures in the 8-week baseline, at least 3 in each 4-week half, and no seizure-free stretch over 25 days. My eligibility checklist calculates each from the diary.

Verify with CRA

A dosing change mid-study

Amendment 1 lowered the starting dose and slowed titration. For participants already titrating on the old schedule, I drafted a question for the monitor instead of guessing.
